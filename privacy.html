Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who I am

Vision Wellbeing is operated by Jack Wells, a CBT therapist based in Edinburgh, UK. This website is located at visionwellbeing.co.uk.

What data I collect

Enquiry forms

When you submit an enquiry form on this site, I collect your name, email address, and any information you choose to include in your message. This data is used solely to respond to your enquiry and is not shared with third parties.

Waitlist sign-ups

If you join the Edinburgh in-person sessions waitlist, I collect your email address. This is used only to notify you when in-person sessions become available.

Booking

Session bookings are handled through Setmore, a third-party booking platform. When you book a session, Setmore collects your name, email, and appointment details. Please refer to Setmore’s privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.

Analytics

This site may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it. Analytics data is anonymised and does not identify you personally. You can opt out of analytics tracking by declining cookies when prompted, or by using a browser extension such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How I use your data

  • To respond to enquiries you send through the contact form
  • To notify you about Edinburgh in-person session availability (if you joined the waitlist)
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it (anonymised analytics only)

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention

Enquiry form data is retained only for as long as necessary to respond to your message and any follow-up, after which it is deleted. Waitlist emails are retained until the waitlist is no longer needed, at which point they are deleted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data I hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)

Cookies

This site uses essential cookies required for the website to function. If analytics is enabled, Google Analytics cookies are used to collect anonymised usage data. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Third-party services

  • Setmore — session booking
  • Google Fonts — typography (fonts are loaded from Google’s servers)
  • Google Analytics — anonymised website analytics (if enabled)

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, please get in touch.

Clinical confidentiality

Information shared during therapy sessions is subject to a separate, stricter confidentiality policy which is discussed at the start of therapy. This privacy policy relates only to data collected through this website.
