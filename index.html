Is this therapy right for me?

I.

Willingness to change

You want things to be different and feel ready to make changes, even if you’re unsure what changes to make.

II.

Open to experimentation

Between sessions, we’ll plan small experiments that challenge the patterns keeping the problem going.

III.

Desire to understand

You want to know why you think and feel the way you do, so treatment makes sense and changes last.

About Jack

I’m a CBT therapist and researcher based in Edinburgh. I hold an MSc in Psychological Therapies with distinction from the University of Edinburgh, specialising in adapting CBT for children and young people.

I work with people of all ages, and believe that therapy should be collaborative, transparent, and provide you with skills you can use for the rest of your life.
